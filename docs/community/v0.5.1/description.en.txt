Turn AI Passport into a handheld controller for Codex on your computer. Press OK to speak, press again to send, and read the reply on the card. Continue the same session at your desktop. Physical buttons let you scroll replies and switch sessions, without wearing a headset or leaning toward the computer microphone, while your phone stays free for other things.

Add and connect your own computer over Bluetooth by default, without preloading your home Wi-Fi. You can also import a configuration file for trusted local Wi-Fi. The card-to-computer control link needs no public relay.

A Windows computer running the companion bridge is required; Codex itself still needs internet access. The new Bluetooth enrollment, per-computer pairing isolation and recovery flows await physical-device acceptance, so this version is intended for people willing to test.
